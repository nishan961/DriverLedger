# DriverLedger — Free vs Premium

**Design principle:** free users are not freeloaders — they ARE the network. Every free driver reporting a washroom, a gas price, or a queue makes the data better for everyone. So free must be genuinely useful, and premium sells **intelligence and tax readiness**, never basic survival or safety.

**His test build:** everything unlocked, no tiers (see ROADMAP). Tiers switch on only at public release.

## FREE forever
- Shift tracking + live net $/hr + multi-app totals (manual + statement CSV import/backfill)
- Basic mileage log (view) and basic trip CSV export — a driver's own records are never held hostage
- YVR + BC Ferries **live boards in-app** (flights, sailings, conditions)
- Comfort Map incl. washrooms & construction pit stops — view + report
- Gas & EV charging prices — view + report
- Airport queue & road-condition reports — view + report
- **Incident Button (safety is never paywalled)**
- Basic HST running tally

## PREMIUM (~$6.99/mo or ~$59/yr)
The "brain" + the tax vault:
- **Push intelligence**: 40-min flight-bank warnings, curb-arrival predictions (30/20 model), final-approach & landed alerts, ferry docking alerts + "make the boat" calculator
- **Positioning Score, Idle-Killer, Tomorrow Briefing**, Rain Money & Event Radar alerts
- **Full Tax Pack**: CRA-formatted mileage log, accountant PDF/CSV pack, quarterly HST filing package + reminders, tax set-aside meter, annual tax-summary reconciliation
- **Watchdog**: pay-check vs BC engaged-time minimum, fare dispute assistant
- **Deactivation Shield vault**: secure backup/export of the driver's full record
- **Insights**: Platform Scorecard, Personal Playbook, Goal Mode pacing, Freedom Number
- **Automation**: receipt-email auto-parsing, Punjabi voice co-pilot, family view

## Pricing logic
- One caught flight bank or one avoided HST penalty pays for a year. $6.99/mo is less than one airport trip's margin.
- Founding-driver offer for the Surrey launch community (first drivers get lifetime discount) — rewards the seed crowd that builds the data network.
